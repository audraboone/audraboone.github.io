Research

My work appears in leading finance, accounting, and law journals and focuses on three connected questions about how firms are sold, governed, and seen by investors.

Mergers and acquisitions

The private phase of the bidding process, including auctions versus negotiations, the winner's curse, termination provisions, appraisal litigation, and internal M&A teams.

Corporate governance

Board size and composition, ownership stakes in subsidiaries after divestitures, specialized equity claims, and bankruptcy spillovers across strategic alliances.

Disclosure

Institutional and index ownership and the information environment, redaction at the IPO, foreign issuer disclosure on Form 6-K, and the CEO pay ratio.
